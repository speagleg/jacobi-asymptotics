## Abstract (plain text for the arXiv form; 1225 characters, ASCII, limit 1920)

Let $\sigma=\sum_k w_k\delta_{x_k}$ be a discrete measure whose atoms $x_k=f(k)$ accumulate at a point with polynomial spacing, $f'(\xi)\sim-pC\xi^{-p-1}$ for some $p>0$ and $C>0$, and whose weights satisfy $\sum_k w_k<\infty$ and $w_k\ge c_1k^{-s}$. We prove that the Hankel determinants $D_n$ of $\sigma$ satisfy $\log D_n=-pn^2\log n+(3p/2+\log(c(p)C))n^2+o(n^2)$, where $c(p)=\frac14(\sqrt\pi\,\Gamma(1/p+1)/\Gamma(1/p+1/2))^p$. Equivalently, a double Cesaro mean of $\log(j^pb_j)$, where the $b_j$ are the off-diagonal Jacobi coefficients of $\sigma$, converges to $\log(c(p)C)$. The constant is the value of a logarithmic energy problem on $(0,\infty)$ with an upper constraint of infinite mass near the accumulation point. We solve it explicitly: the minimiser is unique, it is an average of arcsine laws, and its density is a regularised incomplete beta function. As a corollary, for all real $a>0$ and $b$ with $2a+b>1$, $\log\det_{1\le i,j\le n}\zeta(a(i+j)+b)=-an^2\log n+(3a/2+\log c(a))n^2+o(n^2)$. For positive integers $a,b$ this proves the leading-order behaviour expected by Haynes and Zudilin and identifies the $n^2$ term. The main theorem and both corollaries are formalised in the Lean 4 proof assistant.
