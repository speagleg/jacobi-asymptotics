\title[Spectral zeta values in recurrence coefficients]{Spectral zeta values in the recurrence coefficients of reciprocal
spectra}
\author{Gordon Speagle}
\date{October 2026}

\subjclass[2020]{Primary 42C05; Secondary 47B36, 30E15, 11M35, 37K10}
\keywords{Orthogonal polynomials, Jacobi matrices, recurrence coefficients, discrete measures, spectral zeta functions,
Hurwitz zeta function, Riemann--Hilbert problems, Mellin transforms, Toda hierarchy}

\begin{document}

\begin{abstract}
For an increasing sequence $\Lambda=\{\lambda_m\}$ with $\lambda_m\sim m^p$ we study the recurrence coefficients of the
measure $\sum_m\lambda_m^{-s/p}\delta_{1/\lambda_m}$. For the lattice $\lambda_m=m^p$ and every $p\ne1$ we prove that
$n^pb_n/c(p)$ has a double expansion in $1/N$ and in monomials $N^{-1-jp}$ whose coefficients are the spectral zeta values
$\zeta(-jp)$, in the natural index $N=n+\frac\alpha2-\zeta(0)$, $\alpha=(s-1-p)/p$. The integer layer is even in $1/N$ to all
orders, and the first zeta terms carry exact dressings $\varepsilon_jN^{-1-jp}(1+g_jN^{-2})$. The first-order zeta terms come from a
band-factor functional that is a Mellin--Barnes integral with kernel $\pi[\cot(\pi wp/2)-\cot(\pi w)]$, and the zeta values act as Toda times. For Hurwitz
lattices $(m+\delta)^p$ the offset shifts only the Szeg\H{o} exponent of the hard edge, so $N$ moves by $\delta$ and $\zeta(-jp)$
becomes $\zeta(-jp,1+\delta)$; for $(m+\delta)^p+\kappa$, $p>1$, the pole of the spectral zeta function contributes
$pR_qW(q)\pi\cot(\pi q)\,n^{-p}$. For general Weyl-type spectra we conjecture that the Jacobi coefficients are a decoupled
Bessel--Szeg\H{o} model flowed along the Toda hierarchy with spectral-zeta times, plus cotangent-weighted pole terms, and we test
this with predictions written before the measures were computed. Several of the analytic estimates are only outlined here; we state
which.
\end{abstract}
